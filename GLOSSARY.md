# RePlate

RePlate helps shoppers compare nearby grocery options while distinguishing known information from sample data and unknown facts.

## Language

**Discovery**:
Finding and comparing nearby grocery options by their location and the information available about them. Discovery does not establish affordability, inventory, SNAP authorization, or a verified route.

**Straight-line proximity**:
The approximate direct distance between two locations, without accounting for streets, barriers, or transit routes.
_Avoid_: Walking distance, bus distance, route distance

**Travel preference**:
A shopper's intended choice of walking or bus travel. It does not establish a route or change straight-line proximity ordering.

**Sample store**:
A fictional retailer used to demonstrate discovery. Its illustrative address and other sample information are not verified facts about a real business.

**Not verified**:
A status indicating that adequate evidence is unavailable for a claim, including SNAP authorization. It does not mean that the claim is false.
_Avoid_: Does not accept SNAP, verified not authorized
