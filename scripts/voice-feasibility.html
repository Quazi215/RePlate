Shosai microphone feasibility check

This local check records a fictional question for up to 30 seconds, then checks decoding and mono 16 kHz, 16-bit WAV conversion. Longer questions are excluded to bound cost and waiting time in the planned feature.

Audio stays in memory and is discarded after the check, on Cancel, or when you leave or background this page. Nothing is sent to Google or ElevenLabs. No audio download or playback is provided.

Use Start to grant microphone access, speak a fictional question, then Stop and check. Also try denying permission, cancelling while permission is pending, and backgrounding while recording. Check the browser’s microphone indicator turns off each time.
